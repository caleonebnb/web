Things To Do

Ca' Leone sits at the foot of the Apennine hills, between Bologna and the Romagna countryside — an ideal base for exploring on foot, by bike, or by car. Whether you're after nature, culture, food or simply doing nothing at all, there is plenty to discover nearby.

Your Hosts Are Here to Help

We are always nearby and happy to share our knowledge of the local area — from hidden gems to the best places to eat, drink and explore.

  • Restaurant and trattoria recommendations
  • Local markets and farm shops
  • Hiking and cycling routes
  • Day trips to Bologna, Ravenna and beyond
  • Wine tastings and agriturismo visits
  • Train and transport connections
