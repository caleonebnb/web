Dreamy tiny home nestled in the Italian hills

The Tiny House

A cozy and romantic retreat nestled in the Bolognese countryside, perfect for couples seeking peace, nature and a genuinely restorative escape. Simple, warm and surrounded by green.

Each tiny house is a self-contained wooden cabin with its own private outdoor space — ideal for morning coffee with birdsong, stargazing at night, or simply slowing down.

Guests have access to the shared farm facilities including the vegetable garden, the orchard and the outdoor BBQ area. The owners are always nearby and happy to help with local recommendations.

A perfect base to explore the Bologna hills or the wider Emilia Romagna region — around 25 minutes to Bologna city centre, and close to hiking and cycling routes through the countryside.
