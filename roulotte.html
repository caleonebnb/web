Vintage Roulotte

The Roulotte

A charming vintage caravan set in the heart of the Ca' Leone farm, offering a unique and adventurous stay for those who love the outdoors without giving up on comfort. Fully equipped and lovingly restored.

The roulotte comes with its own private outdoor area with a table and chairs, perfect for dining under the stars or watching the sunset over the Bolognese hills. Guests have access to shared shower and toilet facilities on site.

Wake up to birdsong, explore the farm's gardens and orchards, and enjoy the genuine flavours of the surrounding countryside. A truly memorable escape from everyday life.

Perfectly located to visit Bologna (25 min by car) or explore the wider Emilia Romagna region — from the Ravenna mosaics to the Apennine mountains, there is no shortage of beauty nearby.
